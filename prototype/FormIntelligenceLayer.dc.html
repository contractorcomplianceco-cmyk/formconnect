The implemented visual language of FormConnect — a dark operational sidebar against a bright white workspace. Coral drives primary action and active navigation, magenta marks everything AI-generated, purple carries automation, electric cyan signals connections and routing. Semantic status colour always overrides brand colour.
Logo Usage Approved FormConnect asset — used as provided
Primary lockup · on ink
The default. The wordmark reads white-to-coral, so the lockup lives on the dark sidebar, sign-in and dark exports.
On deep ink · flat
Any surface from #050A18 to #13203C. Keep clear space of one mark-width on all sides.
Mark only — icon contexts
Cropped directly from the approved lockup — favicon, collapsed sidebar, avatars. Minimum 20px. Works on light or dark.
Clear space & minimum size
Dashed line marks the minimum clear space. Lockup never renders below 132px wide; use the mark instead.
Do
  • Use the supplied asset. Place the lockup exactly as delivered, at any scale.
  • Give it a dark ground. Ink surfaces are where the wordmark is legible.
  • Let the UI support the logo. Coral, magenta, purple and cyan accents all derive from the mark.
Don't
  • No recolouring. Colour, shape and wordmark stay as delivered.
  • No redrawing or substituting. Never rebuild the mark as an icon or simplify it.
  • No tight crops. The mark stays whole and recognisable.

Assign a category its colour once and keep it across every chart. Never cycle the full spectrum for decoration. Where a series carries status meaning — completed, pending, failed — the semantic colour replaces the brand colour. Tooltips use an ink background with white text.
Elevation & accessibilitySoft shadows, controlled glow
shadow-sm — resting cards, inputs, badges.
shadow-md — hover lift, builder canvas.
shadow-lg — menus, toasts, overlays.
Glow — hover only, on primary actions. Never ambient.
Accessibility. Body text holds at least 4.5:1 on white — coral and magenta are used at the 600/700 steps for text and at 500 for fills. Status is never signalled by colour alone: every badge pairs its tint with a written label, and connection health pairs its dot with a status word. Focus is always a visible 3px ring, never removed. Sidebar text sits at 4.6:1 or better against the ink ground; the collapsed rail keeps 44px hit targets.
